# ✨ Catch the Falling Stars

A cute, pink-themed browser game where you catch falling stars, collect special rewards, dodge dangerous items, and try to beat your best score! 💖⭐

## 🎮 About the Game

**Catch the Falling Stars** is a browser-based arcade game built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It features a magical pastel design, fun visual effects, sound effects, special falling items, and controls for both desktop and mobile devices.

The goal is simple: catch as many stars as possible, avoid dangerous items, protect your lives, and reach higher levels!

## 🌸 Features

- ⭐ **Star Catching Gameplay:** Catch ordinary stars to earn points.
- 👑 **Golden Stars:** Earn 3 points for each golden star.
- 💖 **Heart Rewards:** Restore a lost life or earn bonus points when your lives are full.
- ❄️ **Freeze Stars:** Earn points and temporarily slow down falling items.
- 🌈 **Mystery Rewards:** Catch mystery items for surprise rewards.
- 💜 **Dangerous Items:** Avoid harmful items that cost you a life.
- 🏆 **High Score Tracking:** Save your best score in the browser.
- 🎯 **Levels and Unlocks:** Progress through levels as your score increases, unlocking special items.
- 🎉 **Visual Effects:** Enjoy sparkles, particles, confetti, floating messages, and animations.
- 🔊 **Sound Effects:** Toggle game sounds on or off.
- 🎵 **Built-in Lofi Music:** Listen to procedurally generated background music using the Web Audio API.
- 🎧 **Choose Your Vibe:** Open suggested Spotify playlists or enter your own Spotify link.
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly Controls:** Swipe to move or use the on-screen left and right buttons.
- 💕 **Cute Pastel UI:** Enjoy a pink-and-purple theme with animated decorations and responsive layouts.
- 🔄 **Play Again:** Restart the game after Game Over and try to beat your record.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Click **Start Game**.
3. Move the basket to catch falling stars.
4. Collect special items for extra rewards.
5. Avoid dangerous items to protect your lives.
6. Keep scoring to progress through levels.
7. Try to beat your best score!

### Controls

| Device | Controls |
|---|---|
| Desktop | Use the left and right arrow keys |
| Mobile | Swipe across the game area or tap the left and right buttons |

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** — Structures the game screens, buttons, controls, and canvas.
- **CSS3** — Creates the pastel design, responsive layout, animations, and visual styling.
- **JavaScript** — Handles gameplay, scoring, levels, collisions, lives, and user interactions.
- **HTML Canvas API** — Renders falling items, the basket, backgrounds, and game effects.
- **Web Audio API** — Generates game sound effects and background music.
- **Local Storage** — Remembers the best score and sound-mute preference in the browser.
- **Spotify Links** — Lets players open suggested playlists or their own Spotify link.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `game.js` in the same folder.
3. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
4. Click **Start Game** and enjoy! ✨

No package installation or build command is required for the basic game.

## 🎵 Music and Sound

The game generates its background music and sound effects in the browser using the Web Audio API. Players can toggle music and game sounds using the available controls.

The **Choose Your Vibe** menu also provides Spotify playlist links and an option to open a custom Spotify URL. Spotify playlists require an internet connection and may open in a separate browser tab.
